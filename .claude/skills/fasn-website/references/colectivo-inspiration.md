# Colectivo inspiration notes (high-level only)

Reference site: https://joincolectivo.com/

Observed public product patterns useful as inspiration:
- Clear ecosystem positioning around a cultural/business community.
- Strong hero with profile/event CTAs.
- A three-part journey: Discover / Belong / Grow.
- Founder/investor/operator ecosystem.
- Profiles, events, membership, content, partnerships.
- AI-assisted introductions/matching.
- Community + real-world events.
- Recognition/mapping of companies.
- Free entry plus paid membership.
- Strong testimonials and social proof.
- “One platform, one login, one identity” ecosystem framing.

FASN adaptation:
- Use Connect / Build / Grow.
- Broaden beyond tech to startups, local businesses, professionals, contractors, mentors, customers, investors, jobs and opportunities.
- Make EN/FR bilingual support foundational.
- Launch in DMV, but preserve national expansion.
- Add service-provider discovery and Request-a-Quote.
- Add government contracting and small-business resources.

Do not copy Colectivo code, text, images, logo, proprietary naming, or exact layouts.
